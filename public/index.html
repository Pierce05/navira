    Rules are drafted from published clinical guidance; clinician review is in progress.

    Questions, answered plainly.

    Is this a diagnosis?

    No. Navira helps you prepare for a conversation with a clinician. It organises what you report and never diagnoses or scores risk.

    Where does my voice go?

    Your voice and story are processed and stored on this device. You choose if and when to export your brief.

    Do I need an account?

    No account is needed to start.

    What languages?

    English, Hindi and Hinglish, including mixing them in one sentence.

    What if my symptoms don’t match?

    That’s a valid place to start. You don’t need a checklist to be taken seriously.

    Is this for people who aren’t women?

    Yes. Anyone with these experiences is welcome here, however you identify.

    What if I have an emergency?

    Call 112. Sudden severe pain, heavy bleeding with dizziness, or pain while pregnant need prompt medical care.
