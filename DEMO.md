# Demo run of show (3 minutes)

Before going on stage: `npm run build && npm run serve`, open the app once online (this caches the shell), and confirm the footer says **Network: 0**. Keep `docs/demo-brief-SYNTHETIC.pdf` open in another tab as the backup.

| Time | What you do | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Slide: the gap between having symptoms and being taken seriously. One figure **with its source and date** (verify first, see PROGRESS.md) | "The gap is communication under pressure." |
| 0:15 | Home → **Tell your story**, tick consent. Speak the Hinglish line live, or tap **Use demo story** (labelled SYNTHETIC) | "Any language, any order." |
| 0:50 | **Looks right** → review chips. Tap **Confirm all**, then tap the two cards marked **Please check** | "Nothing enters the record until she confirms it. Where we borrowed context, she has to check it herself." |
| 1:10 | Continue → skip or answer a couple of follow-ups → **See my timeline** → point at **Not yet known** | "The restraint moment: gaps, not guesses. No diagnosis, no score." |
| 1:30 | **Make my brief**. Show the rambling transcript next to the one-page brief. **Download PDF** | "Thirty seconds for the clinician." |
| 1:50 | Turn on **airplane mode**. Reload. Run the demo again. Open **Privacy**: Network indicator reads 0. Tap **Delete all data** | "On this device. Verifiably." |
| 2:20 | Story screen: type or say `Achanak bahut tez pet dard hua aur main behosh ho gayi` | The calm interrupt appears: 112, Continue later, "This isn't urgent for me". "Rule-based, offline, runs first." |
| 2:35 | What we don't do: no diagnosis, no score. Roadmap: clinician review, local languages, "what changed" | |
| 2:50 | Close | "Every woman walks in with her story, not just her memory." |

## If something fails (fallback ladder)

1. Mic blocked or noisy room → **Use demo story** (no mic needed).
2. Whisper slow on the demo laptop → choose the **Faster** model under "About voice on this device", or type.
3. Anything else → play the backup video, or show `docs/demo-brief-SYNTHETIC.pdf`.

## Judging-criteria map

Problem clarity: the named gap. Innovation: voice + local + clinical artifact. Feasibility: this working demo. Impact: diagnostic delay. Responsibility: safety interrupt, confirmation gate, Network indicator, draft-review labelling.
